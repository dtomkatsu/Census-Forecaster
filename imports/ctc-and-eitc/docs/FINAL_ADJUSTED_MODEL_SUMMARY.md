## Detailed Adjustments Applied

### Adjustment 1: Capital Gains Scaling

**Problem**: Capital gains represented only 1.65% of AGI (below recession levels)

**Solution**: 
- Applied differentiated scaling by filing status
- Single: 1.5x scaling (needed more)
- Joint: 1.0x scaling (standard)
- HoH: 1.5x scaling (needed more)
- MFS: 0.5x scaling (already high)

**Additional refinements**:
- Income-based adjustments (higher income = more capital gains)
- Capped individual capital gains at 50% of AGI
- Preserved distribution patterns

**Result**: 
- Overall: 1.65% → 3.31% ✅
- Joint: 1.72% → 4.04% ✅
- Single: 0.32% → 0.94% ✅
- HoH: 0.93% → 2.07% ✅
- MFS: 7.15% → 7.99% ✅

### Adjustment 2: MFS Growth Cap

**Problem**: Historical CAGR of 55% projected MFS revenue to $1,529M by 2026 (478% growth)

**Reasoning**:
- MFS is only 2.5% of filers (small base effect)
- 2018-2021 growth likely COVID-related anomaly
- Not sustainable long-term
- Industry norm: 10-15% CAGR for stable categories

**Solution**:
- Capped MFS growth at 15% CAGR
- Applied to returns, AGI, and tax revenue
- Maintained realistic growth trajectory

**Result**:
- 2026 MFS Revenue: $463M (not $1,529M)
- Total 2026 Revenue: $4,015M (not $5,083M)
- Overall growth: 45.7% over 4 years (realistic)
- Average annual growth: 11.4% (sustainable)
