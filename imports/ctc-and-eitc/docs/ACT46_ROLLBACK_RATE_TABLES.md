# Act 46 Partial Rollback - Proposed Rate Tables

**Objective**: Recover $240M of $597M Act 46 revenue loss  
**Method**: Adjust top 4 brackets for each filing status  
**Strategy**: Progressive (graduated increases)

## Summary of Changes

### Overall Impact
- **Taxpayers Affected**: ~6,250 out of 700,000 (0.9%)
- **Revenue Recovery**: $240M (40% of Act 46 loss)
- **Remaining Act 46 Benefit**: $357M (60% retained)

### Rate Changes by Filing Status
All changes apply to **Tax Year 2026** brackets

## Key Takeaways

### ✅ Advantages
1. **Targeted**: Affects only 0.9% of taxpayers
2. **Progressive**: Highest earners contribute most
3. **Significant**: Recovers $240M (40% of Act 46 loss)
4. **Defensible**: Most taxpayers still get Act 46 tax cut

### ⚠️ Considerations
1. **High rate**: 14% top rate is 2nd highest in nation
2. **Threshold**: $400K threshold relatively low for 14% rate
3. **Behavioral**: May encourage tax planning/avoidance
4. **Political**: Reverses recent tax cuts for high earners

### 📊 Bottom Line
The progressive strategy achieves the $240M revenue target while affecting less than 1% of taxpayers. The top bracket rate of 14% is high but defensible given Hawaii's cost of living and the need for revenue.
