Caveats:
- TY 2025 federal EITC/CTC parameters used as TY 2028 proxy.
- TCJA expiration (12/31/2025) not modeled. HI EITC ratio change is exact.
- HI EITC take-up anchored to 70% of IRS SOI federal EITC count (estimated; replace with DOTAX Tax Credits Claimed when available).
- LFP elasticity 0.5 (Meyer-Rosenbaum 2001; range 0.3-0.7).
- HoH scope includes single fathers (~10% of HoH per HI ACS S1101).
- FICA + federal-tax savings linearly approximated using baseline values.
- ±15% uncertainty band on behavioral persons-newly-poor from elasticity range.
- SNAP offset (1B) approximates per-filer gain by zeroing all affected filers simultaneously; overstates offset slightly for multi-affected-filer HHs (rare).
- Intensive-margin response (1C) restricted to EITC phase-in workers, ε=0.15 (Eissa-Hoynes 2004 midpoint).
- Bootstrap CI (1D) captures PUMS sampling uncertainty only; elasticity uncertainty via --lfp-elasticity sweep.
- Other channels not modeled: marriage / fertility / take-up of other safety nets (TANF, UI).
